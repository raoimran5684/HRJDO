OUR CHARTER

Manshoor – 10 Bunyaadi Usool منشور

HRJDO works on these ten core principles for justice, protection and awareness across Sindh and Pakistan. Har ghareeb ko insaf dilana hamara mission hai.

1

Free Legal Aid

مفت قانونی مدد

Provide free legal assistance to poor and oppressed citizens without discrimination across Sindh.

2

Human Rights Protection

انسانی حقوق کا تحفظ

Protect fundamental human rights guaranteed by Constitution of Pakistan 1973.

3

Women & Child Safety

خواتین و بچوں کا تحفظ

Special cell for protection of women and children against violence and abuse.

4

Justice for All

سب کے لیے انصاف

Ensure speedy justice and fight against injustice in courts and institutions.

5

Legal Awareness

قانونی آگاہی

Educate public about their legal rights through seminars and awareness campaigns.

6

Mediation & Reconciliation

صلح و مصالحت

Resolve disputes through mediation to reduce burden on judiciary and police.

7

Anti-Corruption

کرپشن کے خلاف

Raise voice against corruption and misuse of power in public institutions.

8

Youth Empowerment

نوجوانوں کو بااختیار

Empower youth through legal education, training and leadership programs.

9

Minority & Labour Rights

اقلیتوں کے حقوق

Protect rights of minorities, labourers and marginalized communities of Sindh.

10

Peace & Aman

امن کمیٹی

Work for peace, interfaith harmony and community brotherhood across Pakistan.
