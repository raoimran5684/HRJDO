OUR CHARTER

Manshoor – 10 Bunyaadi Usool منشور

HRJDO works on these ten core principles for justice, protection and awareness across Sindh and Pakistan.

1. Free Legal Aid
مفت قانونی مدد
Provide free legal assistance to poor and oppressed citizens without discrimination.
2. Human Rights Protection
انسانی حقوق کا تحفظ
Protect fundamental human rights guaranteed by Constitution of Pakistan.
3. Women & Child Safety
خواتین و بچوں کا تحفظ
Special cell for protection of women and children against violence and abuse.
4. Justice for All
سب کے لیے انصاف
Ensure speedy justice and fight against injustice in courts and institutions.
5. Legal Awareness
قانونی آگاہی
Educate public about their legal rights through seminars and campaigns.
6. Mediation & Reconciliation
صلح و مصالحت
Resolve disputes through mediation to reduce burden on judiciary.
